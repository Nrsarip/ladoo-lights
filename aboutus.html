Our Story

Weaving Tradition with Modern Elegance

Ladoo and Lights isn't just about decoration; it's about creating the sacred atmosphere where your new life begins.

From a Single Marigold to Majestic Mandaps

Ladoo and Lights began in Kansas over 15 years ago, rooted in a passion for decorating and a love for bringing people together. That connection to community and creativity has kept the brand growing and continues to inspire every event we design today.

The name "Ladoo" represents the sweetness of new beginnings and the traditional warmth of Indian hospitality, while "Light" signifies the illumination of love and the contemporary flair we bring to every venue.
